How it works

When a transient has photometry or spectra restricted to a collaboration group you are not in, its Spectra and Detailed Photometry tabs show how many datasets are hidden and let you ask the owner group for access.

The data's PIs and the deputies they designated are notified and decide here; group members and group owners do not. Data without an explicit PI takes its allocation's PI; data with neither can only be shared by an administrator, and administrators' decisions are logged. Accept adds the requester's chosen group to the datasets you may share (the same mechanism every visibility check uses); Decline records a note. The requester is notified either way.

PIs: designate deputies who may share your data.

REST: /api/dataaccessrequests/ with accept / decline actions.
