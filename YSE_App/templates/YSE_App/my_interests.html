What this is

An interest is a registered intention to publish on a transient, one per planned paper. Registering one posts a comment on the transient so collaborators see who is working on what, and notifies the others who registered there.

Statuses: planned, in progress, submitted, published (add the DOI), withdrawn. Withdrawing posts a comment too. REST: /api/transientinterests/.
