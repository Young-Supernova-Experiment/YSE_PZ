How access rules work
  • Allow lets a group or one person see the chosen data. Restrict keeps them from it.
  • A rule for one person beats a rule for their groups. Among groups, a restriction beats an allow. Either beats the data's own groups.
  • Shared data stay shared. A restriction is not applied to data the grantee can already see, because they may already have downloaded it. The result says how many items were kept shared.
  • Public data are visible to everyone and are never restricted.
  • A program or night action also covers data taken later. Stopping it excludes only data taken after the stop.
  • Someone who leaves a group keeps what was shared with the group while they were a member.
