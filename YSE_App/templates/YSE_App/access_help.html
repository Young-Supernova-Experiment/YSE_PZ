How access rules work
  • Allow lets a group or one person see the chosen data. Restrict keeps them from it.
  • A rule for one person beats a rule for their groups. Among groups, a restriction beats an allow. Either beats the data's own groups.
  • You may restrict any non-public data from anyone, also data already shared with them.
  • Turning sharing off (stopping a standing grant, turning off an allow, removing a group from a dataset) works going forward by default: data taken before the change stay visible to them. Choose backwards to remove earlier data too; they may already have looked at or downloaded them.
  • Public data, including anything reported to TNS, are visible to everyone and are never restricted.
  • A program or night action also covers data taken later.
  • Someone who leaves a group keeps what was shared with the group while they were a member.
