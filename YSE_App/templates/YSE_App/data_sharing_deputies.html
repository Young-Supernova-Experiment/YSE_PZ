Who may share private data

Only the PIs of private photometry and spectra, and the deputies they designate here, may accept or decline data access requests or change the groups of restricted data. Group members and group owners cannot.

Data without an explicit PI takes the PI of the allocation it was observed under. Data without either can only be shared by an administrator; administrators' decisions are logged.

Public data (ZTF and ATLAS forced photometry, TNS and literature data) needs no PI and is never requested.
