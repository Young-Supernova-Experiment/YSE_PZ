Getting started

This dashboard lists transients by status, one box per status. Hover a status name to see what it means.

To look up one object, type its name or coordinates in the search box at the top. Transient search in the sidebar has every filter.

Click the star next to a transient to add it to your Favorites. Your own lists are on My dashboard.

Not sure what MJD, real/bogus or a Bazin fit means? The Glossary under Help explains them, and the small “?” links on pages go straight to the right entry.
